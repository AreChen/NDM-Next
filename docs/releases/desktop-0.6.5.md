## English

- **Storage and cache** and **Proxy and network** each have a dedicated row in Settings. On wide windows, the summary and quick controls sit side by side. Opening either form keeps its header in place while the cards below move with the expanding content.
- Settings sections ease open and closed over 220 ms. Click again during the transition to reverse it and keep your input. Turn off **Interface motion** for immediate expansion and collapse.
- Choose **Upgrade** in NDM Next or download the package for your platform below. The companion browser extension is version 1.2.1.

## 中文

- 设置页的**存储与缓存**和**代理与网络**现在各占一行，宽窗口将摘要与快捷操作并排显示。展开表单时卡头保持原位，下方卡片随正文高度平滑移动。
- 设置分组使用 220 毫秒的缓入缓出动画。展开途中再次点击即可从当前位置收起，已输入的内容会保留。关闭**界面动效**后，展开和收起会立即完成。
- 在 NDM Next 中选择**升级**，或从本页下载对应平台的安装包。配套浏览器扩展版本为 1.2.1。
