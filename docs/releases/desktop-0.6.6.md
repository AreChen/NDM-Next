## English

- Settings cards in the same row now line up along their top and bottom edges.
- **Storage and cache** shows the current download folder. **Proxy and network** shows the proxy setting for new downloads. On wide windows the two cards sit side by side. Use the arrow on either card to open its form across the full row. On narrow windows each form opens directly below its card.
- Choose **Upgrade** in NDM Next or download the package for your platform below.

## 中文

- 设置页同一排卡片的上下边缘现在对齐。
- **存储与缓存**直接显示当前下载目录，**代理与网络**显示新任务的代理设置。宽窗口下两张卡片并排，点击右侧箭头即可在下方平滑展开整行表单。窄窗口下，表单紧跟对应卡片。
- 在 NDM Next 中选择**升级**，或从本页下载对应平台的安装包。
